# Minji Hunter

Play: https://minjidaddy.github.io/MinjiHunter-play/

Leaderboard: https://minjidaddy.github.io/MinjiHunter-play/ranking.html

Mobile portrait pixel platformer with forest and cave stages, end-of-stage portals, and a shared top-20 leaderboard. Coins award 100 points each. SCORE is at the upper left and a five-minute countdown is at the upper right (minutes:seconds:hundredths). Both stages share the timer, which freezes while paused or transitioning. Clearing the dungeon adds 10 points per full remaining second to the final shared-ranking score; death and timeout award no time bonus. This repository contains compiled game files only. Original development sources and source history are maintained in a separate private repository.

Sound defaults on; touch the screen to start audio. Defeated monsters drop 1-3 coins and have a 30% chance to drop a full-heal potion. Walk over landed items to collect them. X: sword combo, Y: direct-hit magic projectile, A: jump (press again in the air for a double jump and a full turn at the apex), B: meteor ultimate (requires and consumes full MP). START: pause, RESET: restart.
