# Minji Hunter

Play: https://minjidaddy.github.io/MinjiHunter-play/

Leaderboard: https://minjidaddy.github.io/MinjiHunter-play/ranking.html

Mobile portrait pixel platformer with forest, cave and lava stages, end-of-stage portals, and a shared top-100 leaderboard (new entries qualify through the top 100). Coins award 100 points each. SCORE is at the upper left and a five-minute countdown is at the upper right (minutes:seconds:hundredths). Each stage starts with five minutes. Completing a stage banks 10 points per full remaining second. Earned bonuses carry into later stages and survive death or timeout; incomplete stages award no bonus. This repository contains compiled game files only. Original development sources and source history are maintained in a separate private repository.

Sound defaults on; touch the screen to start audio. Defeated monsters drop 1-3 coins and have a 30% chance to drop a full-heal potion. Walk over landed items to collect them. X: sword combo or equipped axe, torch, dagger or shuriken throw; repeated pickups improve throw speed. Shield grants five seconds of immunity; winged shoes boost movement until hit. Lava features moving platforms, jump gaps and erupting fireballs. Y: piercing magic projectile (500-unit range, direct contact only), A: jump (press again in the air for a double jump and a full turn at the apex), B: meteor ultimate (requires and consumes full MP). START: pause, RESET: restart.

Game artwork and sound assets are supplied for this game; their inclusion does not grant standalone redistribution rights. Phaser retains its own license.

Choose Minji (민지) or Cherry (체리) before starting. Two reserved character slots are marked ?. Both characters share the same abilities and combat timing, with separate sword, unarmed and throwing artwork. The last choice is remembered on this device. Character selection is also available from pause and result screens; changing character starts a new adventure.
